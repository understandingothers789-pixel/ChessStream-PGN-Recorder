# ChessStream PGN Recorder v1.6.9

- Accept a supported legal position after 0.5 seconds unchanged, measured from the first captured frame rather than after animation gating.
- Compare frames at least twice per second independently of screenshot saving. The default adaptive rate is now 2 FPS; faster saved settings remain available. Nonadaptive capture intervals longer than 0.5 seconds no longer slow recognition below 2 Hz. Screenshot saving remains off by default.
- Repair recent inferred history when later evidence contradicts a false capture or detour. Bounded recovery retries from before inferred moves within the last eight recorded plies and accepts only a legal sequence reaching the exact observed placement. The same game ID and earlier prefix are retained; affected suffix annotations/clocks are removed and replacement moves are labeled inferred. The diagnostic audit records replaced and replacement moves. Repair uses the remaining gap-search time/node budget.
- Retain captures/recaptures, double pawn pushes, single pawn pushes, then piece ordering. Unresolved recovery errors remain reported at three seconds.
